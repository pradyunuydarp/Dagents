\subsection{F3. Privacy-enhancing technologies for releasing data and statistics}
\label{sec:survey-pets}

\paragraph{Two traditions.} Statistical disclosure control (SDC) treats privacy
as a property of the released table. A methodologist, or a search over
transformations, recodes, suppresses, top-codes, microaggregates or adds noise
until the output meets a target and the loss of utility is
acceptable~\citep{hundepool2012statistical}. Its syntactic models are
\(k\)-anonymity~\citep{sweeney2002kanonymity}, \(l\)-diversity~\citep{machanavajjhala2007ldiversity}
and \(t\)-closeness~\citep{li2007tcloseness}, and its tools include
microaggregation~\citep{domingoferrer2002practical}, ARX~\citep{prasser2020flexible}
and sdcMicro~\citep{templ2015statistical}. The strategies of GRAILS come from
this tradition, and so do Dagents's \emph{generalise}, \emph{redact},
\emph{aggregate only} (a minimum-frequency rule) and \emph{refuse}.
Differential privacy (DP) treats privacy as a property of the
algorithm~\citep{dwork2006calibrating,dwork2014algorithmic}. A DP guarantee
needs a unit of privacy, a bound on each unit's contribution, noise calibrated to
that bound and drawn unpredictably, and accounting across every release.

\paragraph{Attacks separate the two.} Linkage~\citep{narayanan2008robust},
composition of independent releases~\citep{ganta2008composition}, predicate
singling out~\citep{cohen2020towards}, downcoding of generalisation
hierarchies~\citep{cohen2022attacks} and reconstruction from many accurate
statistics~\citep{dinur2003revealing,garfinkel2019understanding} defeat
syntactic protections. A property of one output table says nothing about an
attacker's other knowledge or about other releases. The DP guarantee holds
against any auxiliary information and composes, which is why the 2020 United
States Census adopted it~\citep{abowd2022topdown}.

\paragraph{Who decides.} In every DP system surveyed, a person chooses the
privacy-loss parameter and the unit of privacy: a policy committee for the
Census, the data owner in PINQ and Airavat~\citep{mcsherry2009privacy,roy2010airavat},
the analyst in Tumult Analytics~\citep{berghel2022tumult}, the pipeline author
in PipelineDP~\citep{pipelinedp2026}, and the operator in Sage and LinkedIn's
audience engagements API~\citep{lecuyer2019privacy,rogers2021linkedin}. Software
then derives the noise scale and keeps the budget. The mechanism is fixed by
composable operators (PINQ, Ektelo~\citep{zhang2018ektelo},
OpenDP~\citep{opendp2026library}), by a type system
(Fuzz~\citep{reed2010distance}), by query rewriting
(FLEX, Chorus and Google's DP SQL~\citep{johnson2018towards,johnson2020chorus,wilson2020differentially}),
or by a separate budget service or scheduler (Sage, PrivateKube, DPack and
Cohere~\citep{lecuyer2019privacy,luo2021privacy,tholoniat2025dpack,kuchler2024cohere}).
Local DP enforces on the device, as in RAPPOR and in Apple's and Microsoft's
telemetry~\citep{erlingsson2014rappor,apple2017learning,ding2017collecting}.
Cohere's resource planner is the closest to a planner that decides: it never
touches the data and allocates budget among requests by
optimisation~\citep{kuchler2024cohere}. No surveyed system sets the privacy
parameter from the requester's trust or purpose, as GRAILS sets a strategy.

\paragraph{A rule that chooses a transformation is not a privacy bound.} A rule
table, such as those of GRAILS, Dagents or SDC threshold rules, can be total
and analysable. Its output carries a bound only when the chosen transformation
is a DP mechanism with a fixed unit, a contribution bound, calibrated noise and
an accountant. Generalisation, binning, masking, suppression and exact-count
thresholds give no bound against auxiliary information; at most they give
\(k\)-anonymity when they are chosen to reach a given \(k\), and fixed-level
rounding does not do that. The NIST guidelines for evaluating DP guarantees ask
that the unit of privacy be stated~\citep{near2025guidelines}. Google's DP SQL
checks its minimum-count threshold on a noisy count, so that the threshold
decision is itself private~\citep[\S3.5]{wilson2020differentially}, and the
implementation literature treats the noise source as part of the security of
the mechanism~\citep{mironov2012significance}.

\paragraph{Relation to Dagents.} Dagents's typed planner proves that every
combination of inputs has a decision. That is a property of the policy, not of
privacy loss. Fuzz is the contrast: there the type checker proves the privacy
bound. Dagents's parameters are constants (generalisation level 1, noise scale
0.5, clip bound 1.0) rather than values derived from a privacy target. Its
minimum cohort is an exact-count threshold. Its clipping, which DP needs, has no
accountant behind it, and it declares no unit of privacy. Its noise is seeded
from the request so that an auditor can reproduce it, which conflicts with the
requirement that DP noise be unpredictable (\cref{sec:limitations}). The family
is stronger in every respect that concerns a provable bound. Dagents adds what
the family leaves to others: who is asking and why, per-decision evidence, and
the release of a model.
